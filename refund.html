❄️ Frost Mart - Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

General Policy

Due to the nature of digital products, all sales are generally final.

Eligible Refund Cases

  • Product was not delivered.
  • Incorrect product was delivered.
  • A verified issue exists and cannot be resolved.

Non-Refundable Cases

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Failure to read product descriptions.
  • Violation of product-specific terms.
  • Successfully delivered digital products.

Chargebacks

Unauthorized chargebacks or payment disputes may result in suspension of access to Frost Mart services.
